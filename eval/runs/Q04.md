# What are the trade-offs of parameter-efficient fine-tuning methods such as LoRA compared with full fine-tuning?

## Literature review

Parameter-efficient fine-tuning methods, particularly Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA), offer distinct performance profiles compared to full fine-tuning. In terms of downstream task performance, LoRA achieves results close to, and in certain regimes even outperforms, fully fine-tuned models [1]. Furthermore, when applied to Hungarian PULI large language models across six benchmarks, LoRA consistently matches or surpasses full-parameter fine-tuning on most tasks [2]. 

However, alternative parameter-efficient techniques exhibit notable architectural constraints and limitations. Specifically, Orthogonal Fine-tuning (OFT) suffers from low parameter efficiency at $\mathcal{O}(d^2)$ and a limited capability for downstream adaptation [3]. Although OFT preserves semantic linkages in weight space, it faces inherent limitations regarding its expressive powers [4].
